How to play

  • Landscape only. Rotate the device if you see the rotate prompt.
  • Left control: throttle lever, reverse through full ahead. Lift your finger and it holds.
  • Right control: turn the wheel along its rim. Tap the hub to order amidships.
  • The hull is heavy. Controls move quickly; speed and heading catch up later.
  • Gold plus signs are fuel. Other ships follow reserved routes and do not chase you.
  • A voyage ends on collision, leaving the marked sea, or empty fuel.

Records and audio

Top-five records stay on this device. There is no account and no cloud leaderboard. Ocean audio is bundled with the app and follows the hardware silent switch.

Privacy

Stillwater Voyage does not track you and does not collect personal data in the app. See the Privacy Policy.
