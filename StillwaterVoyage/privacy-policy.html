Stillwater Voyage Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Stillwater Voyage is an offline sailing game. It does not require an account, does not include advertising, and does not include in-app purchases. The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Who we are

Questions about this policy can be sent to tuzzz2685@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app may store the following locally on your device:

  • Local top-five voyage records (survival time and related run stats).
  • Simple preferences such as whether ocean audio is muted.

These records stay on the device. The current version does not upload them, does not use iCloud, and does not create an account.

3. Data we do not collect

Stillwater Voyage does not collect:

  • Name, email, or other contact information from inside the app.
  • Location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings for gameplay.
  • Advertising identifiers or analytics SDKs.
  • Gameplay telemetry sent to a server.

Ocean audio is a local file bundled with the app. The game itself loads from files inside the app, not from a remote website.

4. If you email us

If you contact support, we receive whatever you include in that email so we can reply. We do not use that mail for advertising.

5. Children

The game does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. There is no chat, no social graph, and no account system.

6. Changes

If the app later adds accounts, cloud saves, or analytics, this policy will be updated before those features ship. The current shipped design is local-only.
